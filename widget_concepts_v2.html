Widget concepts v2

Four directions for the home widget. The complaint being solved: the current free-floating map grid runs underneath the text at arbitrary positions, so it reads as noise instead of structure. Each concept is shown as small light, small dark, medium, and a deep-red stress test. Nothing ships until you pick.

A. Ruled card

The grid becomes horizontal hairlines that sit exactly BETWEEN the content rows, like a ledger. Same survey-paper mood, but every line now aligns with the text because the lines are the row separators. Smallest change from today.

My read: C is the strongest total package. It keeps the map-paper identity you built, but the grid finally means something (it only exists inside the map band) and the text sits on clean paper, which was your exact complaint. B is the boldest and your real precinct shape makes every widget personal, but tall skinny precincts will render as a sliver some days. A is the safest refinement. D is handsome but reads more "banking app" than "map". Happy to mix too, for example C's split with B's bigger shape.
