My read: all three carry every field the widget has ever shown, plus the shape and the restored income cell. D2 (corner stamp) is my pick: the map lives in its own bounded corner with the pin tucked in, the shape reads instantly, and the text column is completely clean, it looks like a stamped survey sheet rather than a template.
